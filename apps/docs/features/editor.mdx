Marble's editor is designed to get out of your way. A clean, Notion-like writing experience with no clutter — just you and your content. All metadata and settings live in a collapsible sidebar so you can focus when you need to.

## Writing Experience

The editor opens empty and distraction-free. Start typing and your content takes center stage. Formatting options appear when you need them, and disappear when you don't.

### Formatting

Standard formatting options are available inline:

- **Bold** and _italic_ text
- Text highlights and colors
- Headings (H1 through H6)
- Bullet and numbered lists
- Blockquotes
- Code blocks and inline code
- Links

### Rearranging content

You can move any block, such as a paragraph, heading, image, or list item, without cutting and pasting.

- **Drag and drop:** Hover over a block and drag it by the block handle in the left margin. The editor scrolls automatically when you drag near the top or bottom edge of the pane. You can drop the block while your cursor is in the margins, not only over the text.
- **Block handle menu:** Click the block handle and choose **Move up** or **Move down**. These options are disabled when the block is already at the top or bottom.
- **Keyboard shortcuts:** Place your cursor in a block and press the shortcut below. The block stays selected, so you can press the shortcut again to keep moving it.

| Action          | macOS   | Windows and Linux  |
| --------------- | ------- | ------------------ |
| Move block up   | `⌘ ⇧ ↑` | `Ctrl Shift ↑`     |
| Move block down | `⌘ ⇧ ↓` | `Ctrl Shift ↓`     |

List items move within their own list. Other blocks move past the neighboring block in the document.

### Section jump rail

Long posts show a section rail on the right edge of the editor. The rail gives you a quick map of your post and a way to jump between sections.

- Each line on the rail represents an H1, H2, or H3 heading. Deeper headings get shorter lines.
- The line for the section you're currently reading is highlighted as you scroll.
- Hover over a line to preview the heading and the opening text of that section.
- Click a line to scroll to that heading.

The rail is keyboard accessible. Press `Tab` to focus a line and see its preview, then press `Enter` to jump to that section.

The rail only appears when your post has at least two headings. It's hidden on small screens.

## Publishing and Scheduling

The status chip at the top of the editor shows whether a post is a **Draft**, **Scheduled**, or **Published**. Click it to open the publish options. Any unsaved edits are saved along with what you choose.

### Publish or schedule a draft

Choose when the post goes live:

- **Now** publishes the post straight away. The publish date defaults to the post's current date, and you can set any date up to today. This is how you backdate a post, and imported posts keep their original date.
- **Later** lets you pick a date and time, then click **Schedule**. Marble publishes the post within about a minute of that time.

Scheduled posts aren't available through the public API until they go live. When a post goes live, Marble sends the `post.published` [webhook](/features/webhooks), so a site that rebuilds on it picks up the new post.

### Change a scheduled post

Open the status chip on a scheduled post to:

- **Reschedule** it by picking a new date or time.
- **Publish now** to skip the wait.
- **Unschedule** it to move it back to a draft.

You can keep editing a scheduled post until it goes live. Save your edits as usual, and the post goes live with the latest saved version. If the post has already gone live when you try one of these actions, Marble tells you the post changed and shows its current status.

### Change a published post

Open the status chip on a published post to change its **Published on** date or to **Unpublish** it. Unpublished posts return to drafts and are hidden from the public API.

### Time zones

Scheduling times use your workspace timezone, and the zone appears under the time. Hover over the info icon to see the time in your own timezone if it's different. You can change the workspace timezone in **Settings**. Changing it doesn't move posts that are already scheduled.

<Tip>
  To schedule through the API or MCP, see [Scheduling a post](/concepts/content-model#scheduling-a-post).
</Tip>

## Sidebar

The sidebar keeps metadata and analytics tucked away until you need them. Toggle it open to access two tabs: **Metadata** and **Analysis**.

### Metadata Tab

Control everything about your post without leaving the editor:

| Field           | Description                                                 |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Featured**    | Mark a post as featured for special treatment on your site. |
| **Cover Image** | Set a featured image for your post.                         |
| **Description** | A short summary for SEO and previews.                       |
| **Slug**        | The URL-friendly identifier for your post.                  |
| **Authors**     | Select one or more authors.                                 |
| **Tags**        | Add multiple tags to organize content.                      |
| **Category**    | Assign a single category.                                   |

The button at the bottom of this tab saves your content and metadata. It never changes whether a post is live or when it goes live. Use the status chip for that.

### Analysis Tab

The Analysis tab gives you real-time feedback on your writing with text statistics and AI-powered suggestions.

**Text Statistics:**

- Word count
- Sentence count
- Words per sentence
- Estimated reading time

Text statistics are available on every plan. AI-powered readability suggestions require a Hobby or Pro workspace.

## Content AI

Marble's Content AI acts like a writing instructor, giving you a readability score and actionable suggestions to improve your content.

### Readability Score

Every post gets a score based on the **Flesch Reading Ease** formula, a widely used standard for measuring how easy a document is to read. The higher the score, the more accessible your writing.

### Writing Suggestions

<Note>
  AI writing suggestions require a Hobby or Pro workspace. Free workspaces see an
  Upgrade prompt in place of the suggestions panel. Text statistics such as word
  count, sentence count, and reading time remain available on all plans.
</Note>

The AI analyzes your content and surfaces suggestions in the sidebar:

  </Card>
  <Card title="Vocabulary" icon="book">

  <Card title="Filler Phrases" icon="scissors">
    Cut softening phrases to tighten flow and maintain focus.

  <Card title="Opening Lines" icon="sparkles">

Click on any suggestion to highlight the affected paragraph, sentence, or word in the editor. Make changes directly in your content, or ignore suggestions that don't fit your style.

### When suggestions are unavailable

If the AI service is temporarily unavailable or you've hit the suggestion rate limit, Marble shows an inline message in the Analysis tab with a **Try again** button. Retry once the message clears, or wait a few minutes if you've been rate-limited.

<Tip>
  Content AI works best as a second pair of eyes. Write naturally first, then
  review the suggestions to polish your content.
</Tip>
